Repo paths are given for traceability only; the receiving designer does not need the repository. Everything below was read from the project at the PlutoSim project repository  (branch `new-ui`, release commit `2c2a50d`, **v0.8.0**). No project file was modified to produce it.

### 1.5 Visual style rules (from `SPEC.md` and code)

- **Exactly one signal-filled (orange) element** in a piece of content **[V]** (SPEC).
- **One allowed gradient:** the ink-800 → ink-900 wash on panels. Modal scrim is flat 80 % ink-950 **[V]** (SPEC).
- **No edge lines in the app shell.** Orange edges were tried and reverted **[V]** (SPEC decision 11).
- **Every in-flight readout sits on an opaque ink-950 plate with a hairline**, never bare text over the 3D world **[V]** (SPEC Phase 6, `styles/cockpit.css .ck-plate`).
- **"No hero slogans, no status widgets. Every line is a fact."** **[V]** (SPEC). This rule is for the app; a marketing site needs some headline copy, but the factual, unhyped tone is the brand voice **[I]**.
- **No em dashes in player-visible text** **[V]** (`docs/invariants.md` §21).
- Glyphs instead of colour alone: ✓ ● ○ ▲ ✕ ★ carry meaning alongside colour **[V]** (`ds/Icon.tsx`, screens).
- 3D turntables use studio lighting on `--stage-glow` with a signal accent ring on a rubber pad **[V]** (`app/HangarScene.tsx`).

## 5. Game features

### 5.1 Flight modes (`src/shared/types/index.ts:706`, `sim/control/flightController.ts`) **[V]**

| Mode | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| **Stabilize** | Stick sets a tilt angle; the drone self-levels from any attitude, even inverted |
| **Altitude Hold** (default) | Same as Stabilize, but the throttle springs to centre and commands climb rate; centre holds height |
| **Acro** | Stick sets a rotation rate; no self-levelling. Rates 100 °/s centre, 620 °/s full stick, editable in Settings → Rates |

- **M** cycles modes.
- GPS modes (Position Hold, RTH) are deliberately absent: there is no GPS model.
- Arming interlock: nothing spins until the throttle has been down once.
- **Space** does auto take-off and land (not in Acro).

### 5.2 Physics **[V]**

- **Rapier** rigid-body physics at a fixed **250 Hz** (`sim/constants.ts`, `scene/FlightScene.tsx`).
- **Real control stack:** a PID stack modelled on the Magis V2 firmware's `pidLuxFloat` (`sim/control/pid.ts`), an X-quad motor mixer with saturation (`sim/control/mixer.ts`), and first-order motor lag.
- **LiPo battery model** (`sim/dynamics/battery.ts`):
  - per-cell voltage curve and voltage sag under load; thrust scales with voltage²;
  - warning at 3.5 V per cell, forced auto-land at 3.3 V, motor cut-off at 3.0 V.
- **Air and ground effects:**
  - wind 0–12 m/s with direction and deterministic gusts;
  - aerodynamic drag and a pitching moment;
  - ground effect (up to 1.25× near the ground);
  - optional ambient air drift.
- **Crashes:**
  - detected by impact speed, tilt (70°) and lateral G;
  - props break and fall as debris (`scene/PropDebris.tsx`);
  - a tilt/G failsafe ported from the Magis firmware.
- **"Forgiving flight":** in Acro through the FPV camera, hits bounce rather than end the flight (`sim/drone/forgiving.ts`).
- **Physics tab toggles:** ground effect, battery sag, air drift, recharge, time of day, clouds, wind.

**How a lesson works:**
1. **Explain:** what the move is.
2. **Demonstrate:** the move is flown by the real controller and physics with scripted sticks.
3. **Practice:** the pilot flies it.
4. **Validate:** the attempt is scored.
5. **Reward:** 1–3 stars, based on time, smoothness (jerk), accuracy and collisions. A crash caps the attempt at 1★.

**Progression:**
- XP is 60 + 40 per star.
- Lessons unlock in order.
- 6 pilot ranks, Rookie to Legend (`app/pilotRank.ts`).

### 5.7 Accounts, offline use and data **[V]**

- **Activation keys:**
  - format `PLUTO-SIM-XXXX-XXXX` (`app/activationKey.ts`);
  - the key is given by the Drona Aviation team on purchase;
  - one device at a time, re-checked every 30 s.
- **Offline:** a pilot who signed in online once can sign in offline. Local SQLite store via Node's built-in `node:sqlite` (`src/main/backend/localStore.ts`), with scrypt key hashing. Telemetry is queued and uploaded later (`outbox.ts`).
- **Flight log:** every flight is saved locally and shown in Profile. No export, replay or recording exists **[V]**.
- **Backend:** Google Apps Script + Google Sheets (`src/main/backend/config.ts`, `backend/apps-script/`).

## 10. Known design inconsistencies in the project

1. **Blue logo vs orange UI.** The mark, app icon (BR-04) and macOS installer background (BR-05) use cyan/blue `#38bdf8`. The redesigned app uses orange `#ff7a1a` for actions **[V]**. No document records a decision to recolour the logo.
2. **Two wordmark treatments.** The app sets "PlutoSim" in Geist 652 as plain text. The DMG background (BR-05) uses an older italic Helvetica Neue 800 with "Sim" in blue **[V]**. For the website, use the Geist treatment.
3. **Legacy stylesheet.** `src/renderer/index.css` still holds the old "deep-space, neon" palette for a few pre-redesign screens (mission map, stick indicator, debug widget) **[V]**.
4. **Stale docs.** The README and some docs list 9 lessons / 2 drones / old mission names **[V]**. GAME_FLOW.md has the correct, code-verified facts.
5. **Mission art style.** The mission briefing art (MS-01…08) is stylised illustration with its own lighting. It looks different from live captures (HERO/GP) **[I]**. Don't mix the two in one grid without labelling.
6. **Font in the design PDFs is Saans**; the shipped app uses Geist (user decision) **[V]**.

## 11. Icon and SVG style

- **Interface glyphs (IC-01…16):**
  - 16×16 viewBox, single colour (`currentColor`).
  - Strokes 1.3–2 px with round caps and joins; solid triangles, stars and dots.
  - Drawn because Geist lacks ✓ ✕ ★ ▸ ▾; always paired with a word.
  - On the website, size them to the text (1 em) and colour them with a token.
- **Logo mark:** traced, organic outline (potrace), not geometric. Don't redraw it.
- **No illustration library.** The app has no illustration set beyond the logo, the mission art and 3D renders. Don't invent icon families "in PlutoSim style". If more icons are needed, use a neutral line set (e.g. 1.5–2 px stroke, round caps) in `--txt-mid`.

## 12. HUD and telemetry styling (for "real instruments" visuals)

See UI-01 and UI-02 for both HUD states.

- Every readout sits on an opaque `--ink-950` plate with a 1 px `--line` hairline and 8 px corners (the cockpit register).
- Labels are UPPERCASE 12 px, weight 600, +0.04 em tracking. Values are Geist with tabular figures.
- Instruments:
  - altitude tape and attitude indicator (artificial horizon) on the left;
  - heading ribbon and wind line top-centre;
  - motors, flight mode and battery card on the right;
  - Mode-2 virtual sticks bottom-left and bottom-right.
- Colour carries meaning only:
  - **armed green:** ARMED, OK
  - **caution yellow:** LAND SOON, low time
  - **fail red:** crash
  - **plot blue:** graph lines
- **Quiet mode:** after 3 s of steady flight the HUD collapses to one bottom bar (UI-02).
